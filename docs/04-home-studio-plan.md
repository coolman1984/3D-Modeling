# 04 — Home studio plan: win one use first

Owner's direction (7 Oct 2026). Compete in one specific use before matching everything:
**laying out and furnishing apartments, for an independent interior designer or a furniture shop.**
RoomSketcher, Planner 5D and Homestyler already draw plans, have large libraries, swap finishes,
render photo-real pictures and walkthroughs. Adding "AI" is not an edge; they have it. The possible
edge is a more exact and easier result for one group of people, and that must be proven by trial.

## How success is measured

The same design task given to several designers, with and without Atrium:
**less time, fewer mistakes (things that do not fit or block a door), and a real willingness to pay.**
Each stage below states the task it must make faster or safer.

## The three advantages to build toward

1. **A layout that explains itself** — alternatives with reasons: this one saves floor, this one
   moves better, this one lets the wardrobe open.
2. **Real furniture and a budget** — a shop's catalogue with real sizes and prices; every design
   comes with a shopping list and a cost.
3. **An easy Arabic-market journey** — from the client's apartment plan to the client
   presentation in a few steps, still working locally on the designer's computer.

## Stages (in this order)

### H1 — Draw the real space 🥇
Connected walls drawn point to point (corners join, thickness, lengths typed), doors and windows
that belong to a wall and move with it, room areas from the walls, dimensions edited directly.
*Task:* draw a client's 2-bedroom apartment from its measurements in under 10 minutes.

### H2 — Start from the client's plan 🥇
Import a picture or PDF page of a plan, set its scale from one known length, trace walls over it.
*Task:* from a phone photo of a plan to traced walls in under 5 minutes.

### H3 — Deliver 🥇
Dimension lines on the plan, printing to scale (1:50, 1:100) on A4/A3, the designer's and the
client's logo, clearer pictures; reliable archive and restore; an installer and saving that are
trusted with real work.
*Task:* a client-ready PDF in one step; restore yesterday's version in one step.

### H4 — Furnish 🥇
A library grown by need (the most asked-for pieces first), size and finish edited per piece,
favourites, a shop's catalogue importable with sizes and prices.

### H5 — Alternatives and cost 🥇
Several layouts of the same room compared side by side, each with its reasons and its cost and
shopping list.

### H6 — Share and grow 🥈
Walkthrough and a shareable client view; then floors and stairs.

## Bar for every stage

Each stage ships only when its task has been done in the running program (screenshots sent to the
owner), every check passes, and what was measured is written down. Old saves keep opening.
